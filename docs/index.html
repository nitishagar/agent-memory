Team memory for coding agents.

amem records shell history and agent transcripts on your machine, serves them to any MCP agent, and syncs across your team over TLS. No cloud, no telemetry — open source, Apache-2.0.

amem demo — record, recall, share

How it works

Four pieces, one binary. Everything runs locally until you point two machines at the same sync server.

01 capture

Shell hooks (bash, zsh, fish, PowerShell) record every command with exit code, cwd, and duration. Secrets are redacted before they touch disk.

02 transcripts

Agent sessions from Claude Code, Cursor, OpenCode, pi, ZCode, and Codex are ingested into the same store — your prompts stay searchable.

03 recall

Six MCP tools (mem_search, mem_read, mem_write, …) give any agent your history, notes, and shared context over stdio.

04 sync

Self-hosted TLS sync merges team histories into one immutable union. Conflicts branch instead of overwriting; corrupt data quarantines.

Free and open, all the way down

Self-hosted team sync is free and open source. Two paid seams are planned for later — hosted sync and org controls — and neither gates the MVP.
